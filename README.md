Project Overview

This project analyzes the Chinook Music Store database using SQL to generate business insights related to customer behavior, sales performance, churn analysis, customer lifetime value (CLV), genre popularity, and regional market trends. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that support strategic business decisions in the digital music industry.


Business Objectives

Analyze revenue trends and customer purchasing behavior
Identify top-selling genres, artists, and albums
Measure customer churn and retention patterns
Perform customer segmentation and CLV analysis
Evaluate regional market performance
Generate strategic business recommendations

Key Insights

Rock generated over 53% of total USA sales revenue
Long-term customers spent significantly more than new customers
Overall customer churn rate was 33.9%
USA was the highest revenue-generating market
Product affinity analysis showed strong co-purchasing between Rock, Metal, and Alternative & Punk
High-value customers demonstrated higher order frequency and lower inactivity
